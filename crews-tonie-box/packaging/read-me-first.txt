Crew's Tonie Box
================

Listen to the tonies on your Toniebox SD card, put your own stories, songs and
recordings on them, and keep copies of everything on your Mac.

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 12 Monterey or newer.


Opening it the first time
-------------------------
1. Drag "Crew's Tonie Box" into your Applications folder.
2. Double-click it. Your Mac says it cannot check the app for malicious software.
   That is because the app is not from the App Store and not registered with Apple.
   It only has to be allowed once:

   macOS 15 Sequoia and newer:
     Click "Done". Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down to
     the note that "Crew's Tonie Box" was blocked. Click "Open Anyway" and confirm.

   macOS 12 to 14:
     Right-click (or Control-click) the app, choose "Open", then click "Open".

   Or, in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Crew's Tonie Box.app"


Using it
--------
- Put the Toniebox's SD card into your Mac. Its tonies show up by themselves, with
  names and pictures for the official ones.
- Click a tonie to listen to its chapters, keep a copy, save the chapters as songs,
  or put new stories on it.
- "Make a new tonie" walks you through it: pick the tonie (tag), add sounds
  (MP3, M4A, voice memos, WAV, AIFF, FLAC, Ogg, whole folders) or record your voice,
  give it a name and a picture, and let the magic happen.
- Before anything on the card is replaced or removed, a copy goes to "My shelf".
  Copies live in ~/Library/Application Support/Crew's Tonie Box/Backups.
- When you are done, click "Eject", put the card back into the Toniebox, place the
  tonie on top and listen.

Good to know
------------
- A Toniebox that is online may download the original content of an official tonie
  again. "Keep the tonie's audio ID" (on by default) helps it keep your stories.
- Songs you save go to Music > Crew's Tonie Box.

Crew's Tonie Box is built on teddy by the Toniebox reverse engineering community
(https://github.com/toniebox-reverse-engineering). It is not made by or connected to
tonies GmbH. Toniebox and tonies are their trademarks. Please only put audio on
tonies that you are allowed to use.
